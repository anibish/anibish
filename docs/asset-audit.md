# Source and publication audit

74 PNG files were present in the original `reviews of software consulting` folder at discovery. The folder was renamed to `reviews` during execution; the original files were not edited or deleted by this implementation. Every screenshot was visually inspected using `../design-audit/contact-1.jpg` through `contact-7.jpg`.

- Captures 1–42 predominantly contain review narratives, handles, ratings, countries and relative ages. Capture 7 specifically describes a website SSL scanning script; captures 1 and 42 identify WordPress work.
- Captures 43–74 include repeat-client indicators and, in several cases, small delivery thumbnails and historical price ranges. These prices are historical source evidence, not current offers.
- Captures 49 and 50 appear to show the same review. Repeated handles are repeat engagements, not proof of unique customer counts.
- Capture 45 displays 4/5; capture 46 displays 4.7/5. The selected public excerpt from 46 retains the 4.7 rating.
- No full-resolution project screens, logos, award certificates or profile PDF were found. Tiny thumbnails are not represented as full original project imagery.
- Relative ages are preserved as unanchored source information. The capture date is unknown; exact review dates are not invented.
- Public-looking handles are retained rather than expanded to inferred personal names. No private communications or credentials were observed in the contact-sheet inspection, but publication rights and privacy review remain an owner action before production.

`asset-import-report.json` inventories every source path, type, SHA-256, project identification, delivery evidence, selected transcription, visible rating, date limitations, platform context, confidence, duplicate note and publication status. All imported sources begin as `needs_review`; a privileged administrator can approve or reject each source in the asset-review module. The local public gallery preserves the supplied evidence and has not been deployed.

Original PNGs remain outside `studio`. Optimized WebP derivatives in `public/assets/reviews/` are local assets. The coastal landscape is an original bundled SVG. Product interface illustrations are clearly labelled concepts and are not claimed as original client deliverables.

The master brief supplied additional experience and recognition names. They appear as owner-supplied, pending source verification, without invented dates, client relationships, award descriptions or certifications.
