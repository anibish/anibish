# Owner-review policy drafts

These drafts describe the implemented service. They are not approved policies or a claim of legal review. Resolve the marked owner/legal review passages before publication. No registered entity, tax identifier, address or jurisdiction is invented.

## Privacy Policy

### Information collected

The application records information you submit in enquiries, account and organization profiles, quotation requests, project workspaces, messages, support requests and uploaded files. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

### Accounts and access

Accounts use password hashing, secure sessions and verification links. Studio administrators use multi-factor authentication. Customer workspace access is restricted by organization membership and role. Customer registration is currently gated pending policy approval and launch validation.

### Enquiries and communication

Contact, quotation and consultation submissions are stored in the application database. Transactional acknowledgements and studio alerts are sent through Brevo. Business correspondence uses the info@anibish.com and mail@anibish.com Hostinger mailboxes. Private mailbox access is restricted to the MFA-protected Super Admin.

### Project files and messages

Project messages, requirements, delivery versions, approvals and support history are stored to support an engagement. Access follows project and organization permissions. Private studio emails are not automatically copied into customer workspaces; an administrator must deliberately select a summary for a related record.

### Financial records

Invoices and payment records currently operate in sandbox mode. The site does not collect real customer payments. Do not submit card numbers, bank credentials or other payment secrets through project messages or enquiry forms.

### Cookies and analytics

The application uses a session cookie for authentication and browser storage for interface preferences such as theme. Confirm the exact analytics and cookie inventory before publication. Optional marketing choices must remain separate from account terms and privacy acknowledgement.

### Processors and hosting

The current service uses the studio VPS for application hosting, PostgreSQL and Redis, Hostinger for business email and Brevo for transactional notifications. Confirm processor contracts, hosting locations and any international-transfer requirements before publication.

### Retention and backups

The service stores project and communication histories and creates encrypted recovery backups. Confirm retention periods, deletion handling, mailbox retention and backup expiry before publication; no unverified retention promise is made in this draft.

### Security and rights requests

Access controls, encrypted recovery backups and audit records support security, but no system can guarantee absolute protection. Contact mail@anibish.com for privacy questions or requests relating to your information. Confirm applicable rights, response procedures and any identity checks before publication.

### Contact and review

Anil Bishnoi operates the anibish.com studio. Contact mail@anibish.com or ani.bish@gmail.com. Owner/legal review required: confirm the controller identity, processor details, retention, rights procedures and applicable legal requirements. This is not an approved policy.

## Terms of Use

### Eligibility and accounts

Use an account only when authorized to act for yourself or your organization. Keep your credentials private and use accurate contact information. Account access is subject to verification, organization roles and the launch controls of the service.

### Services and quotations

Service catalogue descriptions provide starting points for a discussion. A quotation defines the proposed scope, deliverables, assumptions, timetable and commercial conditions. Displayed placeholder prices are not binding offers. An enquiry alone does not create an order or a commitment to deliver work.

### Orders and scope

Review the specific quotation and its terms before accepting. The application records quotation decisions and applicable policy versions. Scope changes, dependencies and revised deadlines should be documented through the project workflow and agreed before additional work is undertaken.

### Payments and invoices

Financial processing is currently sandbox only. No real payment is requested or collected through checkout. Sandbox invoices and payment confirmations are test records. Real financial operations require separate merchant, business and tax configuration and owner activation.

### Delivery and revisions

Project workspaces support milestones, versioned deliveries, revision requests and acceptance decisions. Review deliverables against the agreed scope. The permitted number of revisions, review periods, handover requirements and any support period must be specified in the engagement rather than inferred from a general catalogue page.

### Intellectual property and confidentiality

Ownership, licenses, third-party components, confidentiality duties and permitted portfolio use must be specified in the engagement. This draft does not invent an automatic transfer of rights or permission to disclose customer information. Submit only material you are authorized to use.

### Acceptable use

Do not use the service to access another organization without authorization, upload malicious content, transmit unlawful material, abuse communications or interfere with security and availability. Private project files and correspondence must be handled within the access permissions granted to you.

### Cancellation, refunds and termination

Cancellation or refund requests require review against the applicable engagement terms and actual delivery/payment records. Consult the Refund and Cancellation Policy when an approved version is available. Confirm suspension, termination, dispute handling, limitations of liability and applicable legal protections with owner/legal review before publication.

### Contact and review

Contact Anil Bishnoi at mail@anibish.com. Owner/legal review required: approve the contracting identity, obligations, intellectual-property clauses, confidentiality, liability, termination and dispute terms. This is not an approved policy.

## Refund and Cancellation Policy

### Current payment mode

The site currently supports sandbox financial records only. No real customer money is collected through checkout. A sandbox refund or credit-note record is a test operation and does not transfer funds.

### Requesting cancellation

Send a cancellation request through the project support workspace or mail@anibish.com and identify the relevant quotation, order or project. Do not send payment credentials. A request is recorded for review; it is not an automatic cancellation or refund approval.

### Scope and completed work

Review the specific engagement terms, scope, milestones, delivery history and any recorded acceptance decisions. Work already completed, dependencies, licensed components and proposed changes require assessment under the agreed engagement; this draft does not set an invented universal refund percentage or deadline.

### Refund decisions and records

The administration workflow can record a refund request, review decision and related credit note in sandbox mode. Any future real refund must use an approved payment process and accurate transaction references. No fixed processing period or refund entitlement is asserted without owner/legal approval.

### Maintenance and retainers

Cancellation requests for ongoing support or maintenance should identify the agreement and requested effective date. Notice periods, unused balances and remaining obligations must be defined in the applicable agreement and approved policy.

### Questions and approval

Contact mail@anibish.com for cancellation or refund questions. Owner/legal review required: approve eligibility, notice periods, treatment of completed work, refund timelines, consumer rights and dispute procedures before publication. This is not an approved policy.
